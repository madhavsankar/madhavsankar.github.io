Financial document QA & NER

Combined a Conditional Random Field financial named-entity recognizer with question generation and answer extraction for financial documents. The recognizer achieved 92% accuracy; answer-extraction ROUGE-L improved from 0.909 to 0.969.

QCPG++ · Quality-controlled paraphrase generation

At UCLA, developed a text-diversity framework and quality predictor, alongside an encoder-decoder model that generates paraphrases with targeted syntactic, phonological, and morphological diversity while preserving meaning. Trained models were published on Hugging Face.
