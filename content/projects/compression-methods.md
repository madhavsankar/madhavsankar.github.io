Compared network-compression methods on established deep-learning models such as ResNet using accuracy, inference time, and model size, and presented the analysis and generation strategies using CIFAR-10. Also analyzed combined model performance and ensembling, including inference gains through core-kernel analysis on a Titan V GPU.

Standalone methods reduced model size by up to 40× with under 5% accuracy loss. Ensembling improved accuracy by a further 2.5% at the same combined model size. Combining compression methods produced a 215× smaller model with only an additional 1% accuracy loss and up to 8× lower inference latency.
