Developed a Conditional Random Field financial named-entity recognizer that achieved 92% accuracy in identifying financial key phrases. Upgraded question-generation and answer-extraction modules to use the extracted entities, increasing answer-extraction ROUGE-L from 0.909 to 0.969.
